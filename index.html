About

I am an Associate Professor and Vice Dean at the College of Information and Electrical Engineering, Shenyang Agricultural University. I also serve as Deputy Director of the National Regional Innovation Center for Digital Agriculture (Northeast China).

I received my BSc in Science in 2007 and PhD in Agricultural Informatization Technology in 2012 from China Agricultural University.

My research focuses on computer vision, computer graphics, artificial intelligence, phenomics, and digital plant technologies, with particular interests in plant 3D modeling, realistic rendering, and point cloud processing.
